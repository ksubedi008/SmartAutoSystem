Privacy Policy

We value your trust. Here is a clear explanation of what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

Information We Collect

When you create an account, we ask for your name, email address, phone number, and a password. This helps us identify you and connect you with drivers.

When you book a ride, we collect your pickup and drop-off locations to calculate the distance and fare.

How We Use Your Data

We use your information to make the app work. Your name and phone number are shared with your assigned driver so they can pick you up and contact you if needed.

We keep a history of your past trips so you can view your bills and ride records in your dashboard.

Data Protection

We take your privacy seriously. Your passwords are encrypted, and we do not share or sell your personal information to third-party marketing companies.

Only authorized administrators have access to the full database for support and troubleshooting purposes.

Your Choices

You can update your personal details at any time. If you wish to delete your account and clear your ride history from our system, please contact an administrator.
