Terms of Service

Welcome to Smart Auto System. By using our platform, you agree to these simple rules to keep rides safe and fair for everyone.

Booking and Fares

Our app connects you with local auto-rickshaw drivers. The fare is calculated at a standard flat rate of Rs. 30 per kilometer. The final distance is based on the actual route taken and is entered by the driver at the end of the trip.

You agree to pay the driver the final amount shown on your bill once the ride is completed.

User and Driver Conduct

We expect everyone to treat each other with respect. Drivers must drive safely and follow local traffic laws. Passengers must be ready at the pickup location and avoid keeping the driver waiting.

Any aggressive behavior, harassment, or damage to the vehicle will result in your account being banned from our platform.

Cancellations

You can cancel a ride while we are still searching for a driver or shortly after a driver accepts. However, please try to cancel as early as possible so drivers don't waste their time and fuel driving to your pickup spot.

Account Responsibility

Keep your login details safe. You are responsible for all rides booked under your account. If you think someone else is using your account, please let us know immediately.
